# Community Code of Conduct

This project expects respectful, precise, safety-conscious collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with dignity regardless of identity, background, experience, or viewpoint.
- Critique code, evidence, and decisions—not people.
- Welcome good-faith questions and explain browser, privacy, and accessibility constraints clearly.
- Protect private information and use synthetic test data.
- Disclose conflicts, copied material, and material AI assistance.
- Correct inaccurate claims and unsafe recommendations promptly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate privacy exposure, impersonation, plagiarism, unsafe pressure to publish, or retaliation for responsible vulnerability reporting are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

The project owner is responsible for moderation and proportionate action, which may include a warning, removal of content, temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion. Security and privacy disclosures should be handled confidentially under [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md).

No public moderation contact exists yet; this must be resolved before opening public contribution channels.
