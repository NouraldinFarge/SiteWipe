## Direct-cleanup safety decision

The retired implementation remains historical and unapproved. On 2026-08-20, the owner explicitly directed the new **Skip detailed cleanup review completely** (`skipCleanupReview`) opt-in for both Standard and Expert modes. On 2026-08-22, the owner amended the interaction contract: exact pregranted access uses one SiteWipe activation; missing normal-window access uses a durable approval activation followed by a fresh permission/run activation. Its shared safety contract requires every run to:

1. perform a fresh read-only preflight;
2. derive either default `detailed_review` or opt-in `settings_direct` from strict current stored settings;
3. bind the active mode, normalized target, associated scope, source/private context, incognito-access state, complete impact, exact/relevant broader access inventory, permission lease, and downloaded-file IDs into a short-lived, single-use record;
4. in detailed mode, show the complete review and collect every applicable acknowledgement, including the typed file phrase;
5. in direct mode, skip the detailed screen and typed phrase after a separately confirmed Settings opt-in and truthfully claim no per-run detailed acknowledgements;
6. re-read current settings and private-access state, reconstruct the complete bound snapshot, exact-match the prepared mode, and consume the token before recovery or cleanup mutation.

The approved amendment preserves one SiteWipe final activation when exact target access already exists and two SiteWipe activations when normal-window exact access is missing. Exact installed 1.11.57 proved that the action popup cannot reliably own the post-prompt outcome, so ADR 0013 carries the same consent count into the 1.11.67 corrective source without changing the verbatim owner statement: activation one sends and awaits the worker's exact durable armed acknowledgement, requests no permission, starts no cleanup, and opens or focuses one worker-created extension handoff tab in the reviewed normal source window without resizing it. Activation two occurs only in that tab's exact top-frame packaged document after the worker binds the tab/window/URL/normal origin and unique `TAB` `contextId` and returns a separate raw capability only to page memory. It invokes the exact reviewed request from that fresh activation; Chrome/Brave may then add its browser-controlled confirmation. Exact installed 1.11.61 proved that review expiry must revoke cleanup authority without letting ordinary in-session maintenance discard the invoked prompt's tombstone and lease. That record must survive until exact terminal handoff settlement or a real browser-session boundary; complete proof arriving after expiry performs no cleanup, reconciles only the exact preflight-classified temporary patterns, and returns a durable canceled result. Direct-open, duplicate, reload, navigation, replacement, replay, and private/wrong-context pages cannot continue, and a replacement popup remains recovery-locked. Private-source direct cleanup still requires browser-controlled **Allow in incognito** plus pre-existing exact target access; SiteWipe cannot enable or prompt either from that private path. Expert file IDs remain preflight-bound and immediately live-revalidated. Reports must label `settings_direct`, `scopeReviewApproved: false`, and saved direct authorization without implying a detailed review or typed phrase.

The exact 1.11.56 owner amendment/source-candidate review and the later release-unblock instructions are recorded separately. The current instruction authorizes preparing and pushing the exact 1.11.67 corrective source candidate to `candidate/sitewipe-1.11.67`, opening a replacement for PR #4, and re-establishing exact-head CI and CodeQL after the synchronized source/documentation tree, deliberate forward version transaction, complete local automated validation, reproducible source/runtime artifacts, and truthful source-candidate-scoped evidence pass. ## Risk acceptance still required

Even after all tests pass, publication would retain these material limits:

- browser APIs are partial/asynchronous and can finish after a timeout;
- Chrome/Brave treat registrable-domain browser-level storage cleanup as selected-top-level-site cleanup, so page storage owned by embedded third parties but partitioned under the selected site may also be deleted while their partitions under other top-level sites remain outside that request;
- exact localhost/IP cleanup skips browser-level closed-page storage/cache because that API cannot preserve the reviewed port boundary; accessible matching live pages may still be scrubbed exactly, and cookies remain separately host-scoped;
- cookie cleanup for exact local origins is host-scoped and does not distinguish ports;
- websites, Sync, servers, networks, operating systems, enterprise tools, and unexposed browser stores can retain or recreate data;
- permission removal or DNR diagnosis can fail and remain pending for manual/retry recovery;
- redaction reduces accidental disclosure but is not anonymization;
- exact-name marketplace collisions create confusion/discoverability risk;
- downloaded-file removal is intentionally irreversible when enabled and approved in Expert mode.
- the direct setting intentionally removes per-run scope visibility, acknowledgements, and the Expert file phrase; its fresh hidden preflight and live guards reduce scope error but cannot make destructive effects recoverable.
