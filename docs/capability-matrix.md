## Standard versus Expert

Standard mode keeps associated domains empty, local targets disabled unless separately configured, protected/PWA origin cleanup off, embedded-frame discovery off, expanded partition/exhaustive/recent/broad discovery off, on-disk deletion off, post-wipe blocking off, and optional tab/OPFS controls off. Switching to Standard, first entering Expert, disabling embedded discovery, or resetting settings persists the feature off and removes optional `webNavigation`; a later explicit Expert gesture is required to enable it.

Detailed review is the default in both modes. It displays Expert associated, local/IP, protected/PWA, preflight-bound file, persistent-shield, permission-requesting, private, and uncertain scope and requires every applicable acknowledgement. The separate, default-off `skipCleanupReview` setting is available in Standard and Expert after an explicit warning. The 1.11.63 disclosure migration resets an older saved opt-in to false before cleanup admission so the current selected-site and exact-origin limitations must be acknowledged before direct mode is re-enabled. When enabled, entering or selecting a valid target completes the same hidden fresh read-only preflight and durable prompt-pending lease before **Clean now** becomes available. With exact access pregranted, final detailed approval or direct **Clean now** consumes the prepared record in one SiteWipe activation. With missing normal-window access, that first activation instead durably arms the exact approval, awaits the worker's matching nonce-bound `armed` acknowledgement, requests no permission, starts no cleanup, and opens or focuses one exact extension-owned handoff tab in the reviewed normal source window without resizing. A second fresh activation occurs only in that worker-created tab's exact top-frame packaged document after the worker binds tab/window/URL/normal origin and one unique worker-resolved `TAB` `contextId`; the separate raw capability remains only in that page's memory. Direct-open, duplicate, reload, navigation, replacement, replay, private, or wrong-context pages cannot continue. The replacement popup recovery-locks while the armed record is admitted after complete independent event/outcome proof and authority reconstruction or reconciled by expiry/maintenance. Permission events and possession are never authority. The preflight binds target/current settings/context/complete impact/access inventory/file IDs, and the message, approval, and authorization boundaries accept only the exact prepared `detailed_review` or `settings_direct` mode. A private-source run requires **Allow in incognito** and pre-existing exact target access before preflight.

The detailed in-product matrix is generated from `src/shared/constants.js`; public wording must remain aligned with browser evidence and this document.
