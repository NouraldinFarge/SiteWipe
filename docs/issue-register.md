## Priority rule

Any newly observed cross-target mutation, protected-data effect, missing consent path, unredacted private value, or false-success result becomes P0 and stops browser testing/publication work until isolated and regression-tested. Browser, owner, legal, remote, and publication blockers may not be closed by editing status fields alone.
